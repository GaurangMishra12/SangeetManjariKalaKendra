Diploma path · Kathak

Kathak syllabus, year by year

Nine years from the first tatkar to the final stage exam. Choose your board – Pracheen Kala Kendra or Bhatkhande Sangeet Vidyapith – then pick a year to see its taals, theory and practical.

  1. प्रारम्भिकPrarambhik

    Foundation · 2 years · Beginner level

  2. नृत्य भूषणNritya Bhushan

    3 years · Intermediate level

  3. नृत्य विशारदNritya Visharad

    2 years · Advanced level

  4. नृत्य भास्करNritya Bhaskar

    2 years · Advanced level

Year 1 · Pracheen Kala Kendra

Prarambhik Part 1

Taals

TeentaalKeherwaDadra

शास्त्रTheory

  • Oral test only at this level
  • Meaning of tali, khali, avartan and tukda
  • Thah (single) and dugun (double) laya
  • What tatkar is

क्रियात्मकPractical

  • Warm-ups for the head and shoulders
  • Stepping in rhythm (chhand)
  • Tatkar in Teentaal – thah and dugun
  • Two simple tukdas in Teentaal
  • One dance in Keherwa
  • Reciting tukdas while keeping tali–khali on the hand

Everything from the earlier years is part of this exam too.

Year 2 · Pracheen Kala Kendra

Prarambhik Final

Taals

TeentaalKeherwa

शास्त्रTheory

  • Oral: sam, khali, taal and laya – thah, dugun and chaugun
  • Lasya and tandav
  • Reciting the bols of salami and tatkar

क्रियात्मकPractical

  • Neck movements (greeva sanchalan)
  • Two kinds of tatkar in Teentaal
  • Five simple tukdas and an aamad in Teentaal
  • One dance in Keherwa

Everything from the earlier years is part of this exam too.

Year 3 · Pracheen Kala Kendra

Nritya Bhushan Part 1

Taals

TeentaalDadraKeherwa

शास्त्रTheory

  • Key terms: laya, taal, matra, sam, tali, khali, avartan, vibhag, theka, nritt, nritya, natya, tatkar, gat, palta, aamad, paran, salami
  • Writing thekas in thah and dugun; tatkar in thah, dugun and chaugun
  • Lives of Shambhu Maharaj and Achhan Maharaj

क्रियात्मकPractical

  • Neck and shoulder exercises; walking in laya and chhand
  • Two kinds of tatkar with hand mudras in thah, dugun and chaugun (Teentaal)
  • Eight simple todas and tukdas; four simple gat nikas
  • A dance set to a song in Dadra or Keherwa
  • Reciting every item with tali–khali on the hand

Everything from the earlier years is part of this exam too.

Year 4 · Pracheen Kala Kendra

Nritya Bhushan Part 2

Taals

TeentaalJhaptaalDadraKeherwa

शास्त्रTheory

  • Ghungroo – why they matter and what Kathak ghungroo should be like
  • Ang, pratyang and upang; eight eye expressions, four neck and six eyebrow movements
  • Kavitt, chakkardar paran, gat and single-hand (asanyukt) mudras
  • Writing tukdas and parans in taal notation; the Bhatkhande notation system
  • Lives of Guru Narayan Prasad and Bindadin Maharaj

क्रियात्मकPractical

  • Thaat, kasak, masak and kataksh; head, shoulder and waist movement in laya
  • Teentaal: 12 todas, 2 parans, 2 aamads, a salami and 2 kavitts
  • Jhaptaal: tatkar in thah and dugun, 2 parans and 6 todas
  • Earlier items in vilambit (slow) laya
  • Five gat nikas with facial expression
  • Light dances in Teentaal, Dadra and Keherwa; thekas shown in thah, dugun and chaugun

Everything from the earlier years is part of this exam too.

Year 5 · Pracheen Kala Kendra

Nritya Bhushan Final

Taals

TeentaalJhaptaalEktaalDhamar

शास्त्रTheory

  • Kasak, masak, kataksh; laghu, guru, drut and anudrut
  • Kathak and Natwari nritya – the difference
  • A history of Indian dance
  • Rasa and bhava; abhinaya in detail
  • The Kathak costume

क्रियात्मकPractical

  • Ang–pratyang, eyebrow and eye movements in taal
  • Teentaal: tatkar and its variations, a traditional aamad, 2 salamis, 3 kavitts, 4 gat nikas and a gat bhav
  • Jhaptaal: tatkar in layakari, 2 aamads, a salami, 2 chakkardar tukdas and 2 chakkardar parans
  • Ektaal and Dhamar: tatkar, 2 tukdas, an aamad, a salami, 2 parans and a chakkardar paran in each
  • Gat nikas of ghoonghat and matki
  • Lucknow and Jaipur gharana styles shown side by side
  • Thah, dugun, tigun and chaugun of all four taals – recited and danced

Everything from the earlier years is part of this exam too.

Year 6 · Pracheen Kala Kendra

Nritya Visharad Part 1

Taals

TeentaalEktaalJhaptaalDhamarAada ChautaalSooltaalRupakSawari

शास्त्रTheory

  • Kathak gharanas compared
  • Other classical styles – Bharatanatyam, Odissi, Manipuri, Kathakali, Kuchipudi – and folk dances of North India
  • Rasa, abhinaya and its kinds; types of nayak and nayika
  • Origin of taal and its role in dance
  • Terms such as bhramari, pakshi paran, pramelu, chari, stuti and jati
  • Make-up and costume for Kathak
  • Writing compositions in Bhatkhande and Vishnu Digambar notation
  • Texts: Natyashastra, Abhinaya Darpan, Dasharupaka
  • Lives of noted dancers, including Shovana Narayan; essays

क्रियात्मकPractical

  • Teentaal: difficult tatkar, 2 chakkardar parans, a tisra-jati paran, 2 pramelu todas, 3 kavitts
  • A story through gat bhav – Holi, Makhan Chori, Kaliya Daman or Ghoonghat
  • Ektaal: 2 aamads, 2 parans, 2 chakkardar parans and 6 todas
  • Aada Chautaal: tatkar in layakari, an aamad, a salami, 2 parans and 6 todas
  • Sooltaal, Rupak and Sawari: tatkar and 2 todas each
  • Aad and kuaad laya in dance
  • Playing thekas on tabla and nagma on harmonium

Everything from the earlier years is part of this exam too.

Year 7 · Pracheen Kala Kendra

Nritya Visharad Final

Taals

TeentaalSawari (15)SooltaalRupak+ any four of Matt, Pancham Sawari, Ashtamangal, Shikhar, Lakshmi

शास्त्रTheory

  • Two written papers
  • All Indian dance styles, their make-up and costume; folk dances of Central India
  • Dance-drama, ballet and opera; solo, duet and group dance
  • Instruments used in Kathak, past and present; stagecraft and lighting
  • The ten pranas of taal; the Carnatic taal system and converting between systems
  • Combined hand mudras; mudras for jati, the Dashavatar and deities
  • Lives of Guru Kundanlal Gangani, Sitara Devi and Guru Madanlal Koser; essays

क्रियात्मकPractical

  • Teentaal: 2 tisra-jati aamads, 2 mishra-jati parans, 2 pramelu parans and 4 kavitts
  • Sawari, Sooltaal and Rupak: tatkar, an aamad, 5 todas, 4 chakkardar parans and 2 kavitts
  • Gat bhav: Draupadi Cheerharan, Ahilya Uddhar, Vishwamitra–Menaka, Abhisarika
  • Dancing with expression to a thumri and a bhajan
  • Lasya and tandav, nritt–nritya–natya and nayak–nayika shown in dance
  • Aad and biaad layakari, recited and danced

20-minute stage performance

Everything from the earlier years is part of this exam too.

Year 8 · Pracheen Kala Kendra

Nritya Bhaskar Part 1

Taals

TeentaalSooltaalDhamarKumbhBasantShikharAada ChautaalAshtamangal+ theka & tatkar only: Madan, Aada Panna, Chudamani, Raas

शास्त्रTheory

  • Two written papers
  • Ancient, medieval and modern dance studied critically; Indian and Western dance compared
  • Principles of choreography
  • Gharanas of Kathak – origin and features; rasa, sthayi and sanchari bhava
  • Folk dances of East and South India
  • Bhoomichari, akashchari and mandals; the classical gaits (gajagamini, hansini, mrigi …)
  • Notation systems; writing aad, kuaad and biaad layakaris
  • Essays on dance, rasa and culture

क्रियात्मकPractical

  • Stories: Vaman Avatar, Sati Anusuya, Kans Vadh, Shakuntala–Dushyant, Krishna–Sudama, Gopi Virah and more
  • One item in Manipuri or Bharatanatyam; folk dances of North, Central and West India
  • Modern Indian dance and raas
  • Dancing to bols recited by someone else; padhant
  • Ratio layakaris such as 3/2, 2/3, 3/4, 5/4, 4/5 and 7/4
  • Nagma on an instrument and keeping tabla

30-minute stage performance – a thumri or tarana is required

Everything from the earlier years is part of this exam too.

Year 9 · Pracheen Kala Kendra

Nritya Bhaskar Final

Taals

ChautaalTeentaalEktaalJhoomraAada ChautaalGajjhampaJatBasant+ Lakshmi, Sawari, Arjun, Ganesh

शास्त्रTheory

  • Two written papers
  • Classical dances studied critically; Bharatanatyam, Manipuri and Kathakali compared with Kathak
  • History of the stage, lighting, costume and make-up
  • Dance with painting and sculpture – Ajanta and Ellora
  • Mandal, chari, karan and angahar; nayak–nayika bhed; raas
  • Layakari up to athgun, and the Carnatic taal system
  • Thumri and its place in Kathak; a full glossary of Kathak terms; essays

क्रियात्मकPractical

  • All hand mudras of lasya and tandav; abhinaya and its kinds
  • The gharanas of Kathak shown in performance
  • Special parans – Shiv, Ganesh, Durga, Krishna, Saraswati, Badal, Bijli, Kamali, Navhakka
  • Thumri in any five ragas such as Khamaj, Pilu, Bhairavi, Desh, Kafi
  • Layakari from thah to athgun and in ratios, with and without tabla
  • Six stories, e.g. Dadhichi Tap, Daanveer Karna, Shankar Vivah, Rukmini Haran
  • One of Kathakali, Manipuri or Bharatanatyam; five folk dances; two of modern, Tagore, Uday Shankar style or raas

30-minute stage performance with thumri

Everything from the earlier years is part of this exam too.

Year 1 · Bhatkhande Sangeet Vidyapith

Prathama – 1st year

Practical 200No theory paper

Taals

Teentaal

शास्त्रTheory

  • No written paper in the first year – it is a practical exam only

क्रियात्मकPractical

  • Tatkar in barabar, dugun and chaugun, with at least eight varieties
  • Basic hand movements together with tatkar
  • Rangmanch ka tukda (salami) and two “Ta Thei Tat” aamads
  • About fifteen beginner tukdas and five tihais
  • Chakkar practice and padhant (reciting the bols)
  • Simple gat nikas and the gats of bansuri, mukut, gagar/matki, ghoonghat and chhed-chhad

Everything from the earlier years is part of this exam too.

Year 2 · Bhatkhande Sangeet Vidyapith

Prathama – 2nd year

Practical 200Theory 100

Taals

TeentaalJhaptaal+ thekas of Dadra, Keherwa, Rupak, Ektaal

शास्त्रTheory

  • The classical dances of India and the states they come from; a first look at Kathak
  • Basic terms: sangeet, natya, nritt, nritya, laya, taal, matra, sam, tali, khali, theka, avartan, palta, padhant, gat, chaal, bant
  • Four neck movements and fourteen single-hand mudras from Abhinaya Darpan (Pataka, Tripataka, Ardhachandra, Mayur, Shikhara …)
  • Six angas, pratyangas and upangas; what makes good ghungroo and a good dancer (patra)
  • Lives of Thakur Prasad, Bindadin Maharaj and Kalka Prasad
  • Writing taals and bols in notation

क्रियात्मकPractical

  • Teentaal: tatkar up to athgun with speed, and a tatkar with ten paltas
  • Teentaal: eight aamads, a simple thaat, ten plain and four chakkardar tukdas, five more tihais
  • Faster chakkars; gats of nikas, ghoonghat and chhed-chhad bhav
  • Jhaptaal: tatkar in three layas, four aamads, salami, simple and chakkardar tukdas, five tihais
  • Padhant of everything learnt

Everything from the earlier years is part of this exam too.

Year 3 · Bhatkhande Sangeet Vidyapith

Madhyama Part 1

Practical 200Theory 100

Taals

TeentaalJhaptaalDhamar+ basics of Aada Chautaal, Sooltaal, Tivra

शास्त्रTheory

  • Origin of dance according to Bharata's Natyashastra
  • Nine head gestures, eight eye glances and fourteen more single-hand mudras (Padmakosh, Alapadma, Hansasya, Trishool …)
  • Thaat, aamad, salami, tukda, paran, chakkardar, tihai, athgun and laya-bant
  • Tandav and lasya, andaz, natwari, pramelu, the three layas, nagma and kavitt
  • Lives of Achhan Maharaj, Shambhu Maharaj and Lachhu Maharaj

क्रियात्मकPractical

  • Teentaal: advanced tatkar with ten paltas, a paran-judi aamad, thaat with kasak–masak, five aamads
  • Teentaal: simple and chakkardar tukdas and parans; six gats (two nikas, four bhav); advanced tihais
  • Jhaptaal: thaat, paran-judi aamad, tukdas, parans and a chakkardar paran
  • Dhamar: tatkar in three layas, salami, two aamads, tukdas, parans and a chakkardar paran

Everything from the earlier years is part of this exam too.

Year 4 · Bhatkhande Sangeet Vidyapith

Madhyama Part 2

Practical 200Theory 100

Taals

TeentaalAshtamangal (11)Ektaal+ basics of Rupak, Shikhar, Deepchandi

शास्त्रTheory

  • Seven eyebrow gestures, and more single and combined hand mudras (Anjali, Kapot, Karkat, Swastik, Dola, Pushpaput, Shivling, Shankh …)
  • Damdar, bedamdar and chakkardar tihai; lari, pramelu and gat
  • Kavitt, shlok, haav, bhaav, ada, kataksh, kasak, masak, bhramari and gharana
  • Lives of Pt. Birju Maharaj, Sitara Devi and Narayan Prasad

क्रियात्मकPractical

  • Teentaal: advanced tatkar, paran-judi aamad, advanced thaat, four aamads, four plain and four chakkardar tukdas and parans
  • Teentaal: two advanced gat nikas and the Panghat gat bhav; advanced tihais
  • Ashtamangal and Ektaal: tatkar in three layas, salami, aamads, tukdas, parans and tihais
  • Padhant of all the bols learnt

Everything from the earlier years is part of this exam too.

Year 5 · Bhatkhande Sangeet Vidyapith

Visharad Part 1

Practical 200Theory 100

Taals

TeentaalDhamarChautaal+ basics of Deepchandi, Jhoomra, Sawari, Matta, Ashtamangal

शास्त्रTheory

  • Hand mudras for deities (deva hasta) and castes (jati hasta) from Abhinaya Darpan
  • Abhinaya and its four parts; the nine rasas in dance
  • Combined hand mudras such as Kartari, Shakat and Chakra
  • Aad, kuaad and biaad laya; haav-bhaav, mukh bhaav, gat nikas and chhand
  • Lives of Pt. Jailal, Sunder Prasad, Madame Menaka and Narayan Prasad; notation of all taals and bols

क्रियात्मकPractical

  • Teentaal: advanced tatkar with ten variations, tatkar in tigun and chaugun with speed, thaat
  • Teentaal: chatusra- and tisra-jati aamads, pramelu and natwari tukdas, four parans
  • Advanced gat nikas, and the Holi and Govardhan Leela gats
  • Dhamar: aamad, tukdas, simple and chakkardar parans, tihais
  • Chautaal: tatkar in three layas, salami, aamads, tukdas, parans and tihais; padhant

Everything from the earlier years is part of this exam too.

Year 6 · Bhatkhande Sangeet Vidyapith

Visharad Part 2

Practical 200Theory 100

Taals

TeentaalChautaal+ one of Ashtamangal, Sawari, Shikhar+ basics of Lakshmi, Brahma, Vishnu, Rudra

शास्त्रTheory

  • Origin of dance in Indian and Western thought
  • The ten pranas of taal, with graha, jati and yati in depth; laya and how it is used
  • More combined mudras (Samput, Pash, Matsya, Koorma, Varaha, Garuda …)
  • Kathak, Kathakali, Bharatanatyam, Manipuri, Odissi and Kuchipudi compared
  • The eight nayikas and four nayaks of the Natyashastra
  • Sawa and paun laya, zarab, kram laya, bol jati, nritya hasta
  • The gharanas of Kathak, and the Lucknow gharana from Ishwari Prasad to today

क्रियात्मकPractical

  • Teentaal: improvised tatkar with speed, fast and accurate chakkars, advanced thaat
  • Teentaal: chatusra and tisra aamads, a mishra-jati paran, pramelu and natwari tukdas, sada and chakkardar parans
  • Gat nikas, and the Makhan Chori and Kaliya Daman gats
  • Tihais in complex layas
  • Chautaal: an aamad and three parans; one of Ashtamangal, Sawari or Shikhar with tukdas, parans and tihais

Everything from the earlier years is part of this exam too.

Year 7 · Bhatkhande Sangeet Vidyapith

Nipun Part 1

Practical 200Theory 100Essay 100

Taals

TeentaalJhaptaalDhamarChautaalAshtamangalPancham Sawari+ one of Shikhar, Matta, Brahma, Rudra

शास्त्रTheory

  • Paper I: history of Indian music; dance in India up to the Mughal period
  • Rasa theory in Sanskrit literature; folk dances of Uttar Pradesh
  • North and South Indian taal systems compared; the stage (rangshala) in the Natyashastra
  • Western ideas of dance; nayikas – mugdha, madhya and praudha; jatis and yatis
  • Lives of Bindadin, Shambhu and Lachhu Maharaj, Narayan Prasad and Sunder Prasad
  • Paper II: an essay on dance

क्रियात्मकPractical

  • Teentaal: Ganesh paran, advanced aamads, pramelu, natwari and pakhawaj parans
  • Jatis with bols – chatusra, tisra, khanda, mishra and sankirna
  • Tatkar in kram laya from thah to athgun; zarab, bol jati and laya jati
  • Gat bhav: Govardhan, Holi and Kaliya Daman, with clean chaals
  • Abhinaya on two thumris and two bhajans – singing while dancing is expected
  • Eight nayikas and the nine rasas shown in dance; thekas played on tabla

Everything from the earlier years is part of this exam too.

Year 8 · Bhatkhande Sangeet Vidyapith

Nipun Part 2

Practical 200Theory 100Essay 100

Taals

TeentaalRupakSooltaalAada ChautaalGajjhampaShikhar+ two of Lakshmi, Ganesh, Vishnu, Basant, Mani, Chitra

शास्त्रTheory

  • Paper I: dance in India from the Mughal period to modern times; rasa in Hindi literature
  • Ballet; the renaissance of Indian dance – Tagore, Vallathol and Madame Menaka
  • Dhwani, auchitya, natyadharmi and lokdharmi, nautanki, naqqali, sutradhar, karana, opera, dance-drama
  • Bharata, Nandikeshwar, Dhananjaya and Abhinavagupta; Pt. Jailal, Achhan Maharaj and Uday Shankar
  • The aesthetics of Kathak
  • Paper II: an essay on dance

क्रियात्मकPractical

  • Teentaal: advanced aamads, natwari and pramelu tukdas; pakhawaj, badhaiya, farmaishi and kamali parans
  • Kavitt and chhand; jati parans
  • Tatkar in kram laya from naugun to solahgun; advanced zarab and layabant
  • Gat bhav: Makhan Chori, Madan Dahan and Ahilya Uddhar
  • Abhinaya on thumris, a bhajan and a ghazal
  • Dancing alone to theka or nagma; nayikas – swakiya, parkiya, samanya

Everything from the earlier years is part of this exam too.
