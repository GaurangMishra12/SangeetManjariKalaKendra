Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

This page explains what information the Sangeet Manjari Kala Kendra website collects and what we do with it. We keep it simple: we only collect what you choose to send us.

1. What we collect

When you send an enquiry, book a seat for an event or register for a workshop, the form sends us:

  • your name and phone/WhatsApp number,
  • where you would like to learn and what you are interested in (if you choose),
  • any message you write, and the page you sent it from.

If you answer the “Find your level” quiz and then book a trial class, your answers are added to the message so we can suggest the right level.

2. How we use it

The enquiry is saved in a private Google Sheet belonging to the Kendra and sent to our email, and your WhatsApp opens with the same details so you can send them to us. We use this information only to reply to you about classes, events and workshops. We do not sell or share it with anyone for marketing.

3. Cookies and browser storage

The website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. It stores two small notes in your browser for the current visit only, so that the enquiry popup and the Riyaz Room suggestion are not shown again and again.

4. Other services on our pages

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, and our performance videos are shown using Instagram's embedded player. These services may receive basic technical information (such as your IP address) when the page loads, under their own privacy policies.

5. How long we keep it, and your choices

We keep enquiries only as long as we need them to stay in touch about classes. You can ask us at any time to correct or delete your details by writing to or messaging .

6. Children

Enquiries for children should be sent by a parent or guardian.

7. Changes

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
