02 — Footwork & spins

तत्कार · चक्करFeet that keep time

Tatkar builds clean, even footwork that lands exactly on the beat. Chakkar adds fast spins with balance and a steady finish on sam – the first beat of the cycle.

03 — Hands & expression

हस्तक · अभिनयTelling the story

Hastak and mudras shape the lines of the body, and abhinaya brings a story alive in the eyes and face – Krishna's mischief, Radha's longing, a festival in the village.

04 — Rhythm & recitation

लयकारी · पढ़न्तLiving inside the taal

Students recite the bols aloud before they dance them and learn to play with tempo. Watch teentaal's sixteen beats move around the cycle – clap on X, 2 and 3, wave on 0.
