      How we teach

      The Sangeet Manjari way

      1. १

        Bols before the dance

        Every piece begins with reciting its bols to the taal, so the feet know the rhythm before they move.

      2. २

        A performing teacher

      3. ३

        A syllabus with a goal

        Training follows the Bhatkhande Sangeet Vidyapeeth and Pracheen Kala Kendra syllabus, so regular practice leads to recognised diplomas.

      4. ४

        Small batches, every age

        Children and adults learn in small groups, where each student is watched, corrected and encouraged.
